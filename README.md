# BME Paper Skill

A personalized academic skill repository for biomedical engineering paper writing, AI in healthcare research, medical data analysis, scientific visualization, and thesis-level academic polishing.

This repository is adapted from `Yuan1z0825/nature-skills` and reorganized for my own academic background in biomedical engineering. The current version focuses on biomedical research writing, healthcare AI methodology design, literature review development, scientific figure design, and paper-level language refinement.

## Background

My academic background is in Biomedical Engineering, with research interests extending across AI in healthcare, medical signal processing, medical imaging, neurorehabilitation, digital health, rehabilitation engineering, and clinical data analysis.

This repository is designed to support the development of high-quality academic papers, thesis chapters, research proposals, methodology sections, literature reviews, and scientific figures in biomedical engineering and healthcare-related research.

It is not limited to a single topic such as EEG or motor imagery. Instead, it aims to build a reusable academic workflow for broader biomedical engineering research, especially where engineering methods, medical data, and healthcare applications intersect.
